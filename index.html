Privacy Policy for ProWeather

Last updated: January 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ProWeather ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

ProWeather is designed to prioritize user privacy. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Location Data:

  • When you grant location permission, we use your device's GPS coordinates solely to fetch weather data for your current location.
  • Location data is processed locally on your device and sent directly to the OpenWeatherMap API.
  • We do not store or track your location history. This data is only shared with OpenWeatherMap to provide the service you requested.

Search History:

  • City search history is stored locally on your device only.
  • This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

2. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use the following third-party service to provide app functionality:

  • OpenWeatherMap API: Used to provide weather forecasts. Your coordinates or searched city names are sent to their servers. View their policy here: OpenWeatherMap Privacy Policy.

3. DATA SECURITY

All data is processed locally on your device. We do not maintain external servers that collect or store user information, which significantly reduces the risk of data breaches.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

ProWeather does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

5. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
